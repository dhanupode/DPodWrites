Case study: Wiki delete-a-thon (org-wide)

Legacy wiki pages were degrading Metamate's answer quality and cluttering search results. I proposed and led an org-wide delete-a-thon effort across Doc Engineering and DDAI, targeting roughly 2,700 people. I built the supporting infrastructure myself: an impact dashboard, a delete-a-thon FAQ wiki and Q&A group, a communications and kickoff template, and a tiered "Dead Wiki Society" badge program to drive engineer participation.

Case study: MetaGen wiki dedup

Metamate was actively serving hundreds of outdated and duplicated wiki pages to users, and since agents are only as good as the content they retrieve, that stale content was degrading answer quality. I audited the full MetaGen documentation footprint, used AI to classify every page into an action category (delete, merge, move, or route to an engineer), and partnered with engineering SMEs to action each one.

546pages audited
288stale pages removed (53% reduction)
16,665stale AI retrievals eliminated
~3 weeksfrom audit to delivery

Case study: Skill delete-a-thon (design)

Internal AI skills (Meta's internal Claude Skills framework) were accumulating faster than they were being retired, adding token overhead and stale tooling to LLM workflows. Building on the wiki delete-a-thon model, I designed a scalable framework to identify skills for retirement based on ownership gaps, usage decay, and disable rate. The framework was reviewed and signed off by senior IC and cross-functional leadership; execution and rollout metrics are still pending.
